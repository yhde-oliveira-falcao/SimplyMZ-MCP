### M1.T7 — integrate_peak
Status: VERIFIED
Commit: 0beac14
What was built: integration with user or automatic bounds (Savitzky-Golay-smoothed walk on a detrended trace to max(3·noise, 0.1% apex), noise-aware valley rule requiring a real descent, extension to baseline), baseline from medians of flanking points, signed trapezoid area, height, FWHM, asymmetry at 10% with parabolic apex, SNR, overlap/edge/coarse-sampling warnings. Files: src/simplymz_mcp/analytics/integrate.py, src/simplymz_mcp/tools/chromatogram.py
Tests added: tests/unit/test_chromatogram.py (integration part; 15 total in file)
Task verification: `uv run pytest tests/unit/test_chromatogram.py -q` → 15 passed
Full fast suite after task: 180 passed
Lint/type: ruff ✅ mypy ✅
Acceptance criteria touched: M1-AC4
Deviations / notes: Measured over 300 noise realisations before writing tests. First design: −17% bias, outliers to 100% (bounds on tails, noise valleys, drifting global threshold, clipping bias); final: SNR 50 bias +0.05% sd 1.5% (95.7% within 3%), SNR 100 max 2.7%, SNR 1000 max 0.3%. The plan's "±3% at SNR ≥ 50" cannot hold for every noise realisation at RMS-SNR 50, so the unit test asserts |bias| < 0.5% and ≥ 95% within 3% at SNR 50, and 100% within 3% at SNR ≥ 100. **Accepted by Yuri (2026-10-05) on condition that users are told** → ADR 0008: every integrate_peak result discloses area precision for its SNR tier, the SNR definition (RMS, not USP) and the validation conditions; same text in the tool description.
